# Decision Log

This file records settled decisions and their reasoning so collaborators do not repeatedly reopen them.

## D001 — The active solo artist identity will become Innercha

Status: **Decided**

Innercha has persisted as an idea for roughly one to two years and feels more evocative, distinctive, expandable, and personally meaningful than SubRoot. It is rooted in inertia, the sound of Kercha, and the concept of inner motion or momentum.

## D002 — SubRoot will not be erased

Status: **Decided**

SubRoot represents years of music, relationships, releases, and growth. The transition should honor that history rather than conceal it.

## D003 — Innercha and SubRoot will not remain competing active solo identities

Status: **Decided**

Innercha is the active future of the same solo career. Maintaining both indefinitely would split attention, listeners, and professional identity.

## D004 — Preserve established accounts where practical

Status: **Decided**

SoundCloud, Instagram, Facebook artist presence, Bandcamp, YouTube, and similar accounts should generally evolve in place to retain followers, relationships, comments, and history.

## D005 — Spotify will use the controlled Innercha destination

Status: **Decided in principle**

Both Spotify for Artists pages are claimed. New releases will go to Innercha. The self-controlled catalog may be selectively migrated and mapped to the controlled Innercha page. DistroKid Fixer is available if mapping is wrong.

## D006 — Do not test a Spotify rename on one arbitrary old release

Status: **Decided**

A partial experiment could split the catalog or create mapping confusion. Any catalog migration should be prepared as an intentional core transition.

## D007 — Only the core self-controlled catalog needs near-term migration attention

Status: **Decided**

Old label releases, remixes, and complex collaborations can remain credited to SubRoot unless updating them is easy and worthwhile. This reduces effort without erasing history.

## D008 — Infinite Edges may remain SubRoot

Status: **Current preference**

The Shanti Planti release is a legitimate milestone from the SubRoot era. Jeff can mention the rebrand naturally when reconnecting with the label, especially around a future pitch, without making a metadata change the reason for contact.

## D009 — Plateau is the bridge release

Status: **Decided**

Plateau already established the Innercha Spotify entity. It should remain part of the Innercha history and can be added to the established SoundCloud account when appropriate.

## D010 — The established SoundCloud page becomes Innercha

Status: **Decided**

The established page has the audience and history. Old uploads remain intact. The placeholder account should not be deleted until the preferred handle is successfully transferred and all useful material is backed up.

## D011 — Instagram evolves in place

Status: **Decided**

The account is hybrid personal/artist but primarily music-facing. A new account would discard relationships and audience. A likely temporary display name is `Jeff | Innercha`.

## D012 — Use a gradual public bridge

Status: **Decided**

Temporary wording such as `formerly SubRoot` should connect the identities for several months, then be removed when no longer useful.

## D013 — Brand direction is understated and expandable

Status: **Directional, not finalized**

The identity should feel modern, organic, introspective, atmospheric, and premium—not narrowly “bass,” aggressive, or dependent on neon/glitch clichés.

## D014 — The repo stays lightweight

Status: **Decided**

This is a creative operations notebook, not a full application. Avoid unnecessary boards, CI, infrastructure, and automation.
