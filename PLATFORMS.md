## SoundCloud

### Desired result

The established SubRoot page becomes the canonical Innercha account. Its old tracks, followers, plays, comments, and history remain visible.

### Safe handle handoff

1. Back up Plateau audio, artwork, description, stats, comments, and links from the placeholder account.
2. Choose a harmless fallback handle for the placeholder.
3. Change the placeholder handle without deleting the account.
4. Check from a private browser whether the preferred handle is actually available.
5. Claim the preferred handle from the established account immediately if available.
6. If it is not available, keep both accounts intact and wait rather than improvising destructively.
7. Update the established account's display name, bio, avatar, banner, and outbound links.
8. Upload Plateau to the established account when the timing is useful.
9. Archive or clearly label the placeholder only after the new canonical page is confirmed.

### Transitional naming

- Early preparation: `SubRoot | Innercha`
- Public bridge: `Innercha — formerly SubRoot`
- Mature state: `Innercha`
