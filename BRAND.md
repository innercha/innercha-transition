# Innercha Brand Worksheet

This is the working identity document for Phase 2. It should guide visuals and language without over-defining the music.

## Core meaning

**Innercha** draws from:

- inertia
- inner motion
- inner momentum
- the phonetic influence of Kercha
- movement through internal, emotional, and psychedelic spaces

Public explanations do not need to unpack every syllable. A concise meaning such as **“inspired by the idea of inner motion”** preserves clarity and mystery.

## Working identity statement

Innercha is Jeff's evolving electronic music project: an exploration of inner motion through detailed sound, shifting rhythm, atmosphere, and psychedelic movement.

This is a draft, not final public copy.

## Brand attributes

Select and refine five to seven:

- introspective
- kinetic
- organic
- psychedelic
- warm
- curious
- detailed
- atmospheric
- intelligent without feeling cold
- ancient-meets-future
- understated
- expansive

## What Innercha is not

- A name that locks the music to one bass subgenre
- Aggressive branding for its own sake
- Generic neon “EDM” imagery
- A fictional persona disconnected from Jeff
- An attempt to deny the SubRoot era
- A promise that every release sounds the same

## Creative territory

Possible recurring themes:

- inner landscapes
- motion and stillness
- inertia and transformation
- liminal spaces
- emergence
- mist, forest, stone, water, sky, and topography
- organic systems and subtle geometry
- consciousness, curiosity, and wonder
- detailed movement at multiple scales

## Visual direction

### Working mood

Premium, atmospheric, restrained, nature-informed, contemporary, and quietly psychedelic.

### Candidate palette

- charcoal and near-black
- fog blue
- muted forest or moss green
- sandstone
- dusk orange or restrained amber
- off-white

Keep the palette flexible until real artwork tests prove what works.

### Typography

Explore:

- clean geometric sans serif
- elegant grotesk or neo-grotesk
- restrained condensed display face for lineups
- simple title case or all caps: `Innercha` / `INNERCHA`

Avoid forced capitalization and novelty lettering that harms readability.

### Imagery

Explore:

- atmospheric landscapes
- topographic contours
- macro natural textures
- fog, moonlight, water, and mineral forms
- subtle motion blur or flow fields
- restrained geometric overlays

## Logo approach

Start with a strong wordmark before designing a symbol. The name already has useful rhythm and letter shape. A mark should emerge from repeated use rather than become a launch blocker.

## Voice

The written voice should be:

- thoughtful
- direct
- curious
- confident without hype
- personal without oversharing
- artist-led rather than marketing-led

## Draft short descriptions

Electronic music shaped by inner motion, psychedelic detail, and evolving rhythm.

### Simple meaning answer

Innercha is inspired by the idea of inner motion.

### Transitional descriptor

Innercha is the next chapter of Jeff's solo project, formerly released as SubRoot.
