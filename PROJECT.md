# Project Handoff: SubRoot to Innercha

## Purpose

This repository documents the transition of Jeff's solo artist project from **SubRoot** to **Innercha**.

The goal is not to erase SubRoot. The goal is to preserve its catalog, audience, reputation, and history while allowing the project to continue under a more distinctive and expandable long-term identity.

This file is the concise source of truth for Jeff, Codex, Claude, ChatGPT, and other collaborators.

## Background

Jeff is an Arizona-based electronic music producer whose solo catalog spans experimental bass, psychedelic bass, halftime, drum and bass, ambient, glitch hop, lo-fi, and related styles. He has built a modest reputation as SubRoot, played events including June Jam, and released music through labels including Shanti Planti.

SubRoot was originally a descriptive combination of sub-bass, roots, and musical root notes. Over time it began to feel genre-bound, less distinctive, and harder to communicate verbally. Multiple artists also use similar or identical names.

Innercha has remained compelling for roughly one to two years. It draws from **inertia**, a phonetic influence from **Kercha**, and the central concept of **inner motion** or **inner momentum**. It feels broader, more evocative, more visually natural on lineups, and better suited to a multi-lane creative career.

Jeff previously released the EP **Plateau** as a SubRoot × Innercha collaboration to reserve the Innercha identity. That created an existing Innercha Spotify artist page.

## Goals

- Establish Innercha as the active solo artist identity.
- Preserve the visible history and reputation of SubRoot.
- Carry existing listeners forward with minimal confusion.
- Keep established pages and audiences wherever practical.
- Consolidate the core catalog without chasing every historical credit.
- Release all future solo music as Innercha.
- Build a current, understated, expandable visual identity.
- Execute the transition gradually and professionally.

## Non-goals

- Erasing SubRoot from the internet.
- Retitling old tracks or replacing their artwork merely to hide the former name.
- Updating every old remix, compilation, or label-controlled release immediately.
- Maintaining SubRoot and Innercha as two active competing solo projects.
- Turning this repository into a large software project.

## Design principles

Every recommendation should support these principles:

1. Preserve history.
2. Reduce listener and industry confusion.
3. Think in years, not days.
4. Avoid unnecessary work and complexity.
5. Keep creative momentum.
6. Favor evolution over replacement.
7. Prefer reversible, low-risk steps before public or metadata changes.
8. Let the music define the name rather than the name define the genre.

## Current status

### Identity

- Decision made: the active solo project will become **Innercha**.
- Name exploration and lineup visualization are complete.
- Outside-scene listener test was positive; the inertia association was independently recognizable.

### Spotify and DistroKid

- DistroKid remains the distributor.
- DistroKid supports full artist-name changes through release metadata edits.
- Both the SubRoot and Innercha Spotify for Artists teams have been claimed.
- The existing Innercha page came from the Plateau release.
- DistroKid's Fixer can be used if releases map to the wrong Spotify page.
- Self-controlled releases are the only catalog migration that needs near-term consideration.
- Followers may not transfer if Spotify creates or uses a different artist entity; release play counts and playlist associations are more likely to remain with unchanged ISRCs, but store behavior is not guaranteed.

### Catalog

- A Google Sheet inventory exists.
- The catalog includes self-released DistroKid releases, label releases, remixes, collaborations, and Plateau.
- The preferred scope is selective: migrate the core self-released catalog where useful; leave most old label releases and complicated credits alone.
- Infinite Edges may remain credited to SubRoot. Shanti Planti can be informed naturally during a future release conversation.

### SoundCloud

- The established SubRoot account has the valuable audience and should become the main Innercha account.
- Old uploads remain as historical work; they do not need to be renamed.
- A small placeholder Innercha account currently hosts Plateau and may hold the preferred handle.
- The handle transfer must be tested carefully before retiring the placeholder page.
- Plateau can be uploaded fresh to the established account when appropriate.

### Social accounts

- Instagram is a hybrid personal/artist account but is primarily music-facing.
- Preserve the existing Instagram audience and evolve that account rather than creating a new one.
- A likely transitional display name is `Jeff | Innercha`.
- The Facebook personal profile and artist Page should be treated separately.
- Other established artist pages should generally be renamed rather than recreated.

### Assets and contact

- `innerchasound@gmail.com` is already secured.
- Visual identity work has not been finalized.
- A fake festival poster showed that INNERCHA sits naturally among established bass-music names.
