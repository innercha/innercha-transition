# Labels and Collectives

Tracking for outreach candidates for the upcoming Innercha album (~10-11 tracks, currently on a private SoundCloud playlist, psychedelic/experimental bass with 110/140/halftime BPMs and light woven world-instrument influence — reference points: ATYYA, CØNTRA, Of the Trees, PEEKABOO). Open to leaning into darker 140 in the near future, so that lane stays live rather than sidelined.

Sound-fit and submission details below were checked 2026-07-29. Label rosters, submission windows, and contact addresses change — verify directly before sending anything.

## Strong candidates

### Gravitas Recordings

- Austin, TX. "Doing good with good music." Organic bass/downtempo/experimental, frequently blends world instrumentation with electronic production — a close match for "psychedelic, some woven ethnic influence, not too heavy."
- Roster includes Of The Trees, CloZee, AHEE, Entangled Mind (whose own description — "psychedelic release... Glitch-Hop and Downtempo bass" — reads very close to your sound).
- **Currently accepting demos.** Submission form at gravitasrecordings.com/demos — prefers a private SoundCloud playlist link (which you already have), unreleased music only, plus a short note on why you want to work with them.
- Priority: high. Direct sonic and roster overlap with Of The Trees, one of your named references.

### Desert Trax

- Founded 2005 by Amani Friend and Treavor Moontribe of Desert Dwellers. Tagline "Music Beyond Borders" — psychedelic and organic bass, "ancient future production and world music." One of the closest matches for the sound as described.
- Where ATYYA has released ("known for organic and psychedelic bass contributions").
- No demo submission page found in research — reach out via Bandcamp (deserttrax.bandcamp.com) or Facebook to ask about submitting.
- Priority: high on fit, confirm the submission path before sending anything.

### Cyclops Recordings

- Founded 2019 by Subtronics. Independent, explicitly built as "a platform for the forward-thinkers in bass music" who support artists "not afraid to be different." Its launch compilation had a dedicated **Psychedelic Division** — a real signal they have appetite for exactly this lane, not just straight dubstep.
- Good fit specifically if some of the album leans into darker/heavier 140 while keeping the psychedelic element — matches what you said about being open to that direction.
- No demo process confirmed yet — check cyclopsrecordings or Subtronics' channels directly.
- Priority: high, especially for the heavier tracks in the batch.

## Good fit, currently closed

### Merkaba Music

- "Hybrid organic electronic soundscapes" blending the natural world with production — strong aesthetic match. Also where ATYYA released the Aurical EP.
- **Not accepting demos as of 2026-07-29** ("not accepting demos until further notice," per merkabamusic.com). Worth revisiting periodically rather than submitting now.

## Dark 140 / dubstep lane

Real, active labels for if some of the album leans heavier or more straight-140 than psychedelic-organic. Reached via reference-artist connections (CØNTRA, PEEKABOO).

- **DUPLOC** — Belgium-based dubstep/140 label, covered by UKF (a strong quality signal in this scene). No public demo instructions found; UKF also runs its own submission portal (ukf.com/submit-your-demo).
- **Deep Dark & Dangerous** — New Zealand-based deep dubstep label founded by the duo Truth. Demo email confirmed: demo@deepdarkdangerous.com.
- **Deadbeats** — Zeds Dead's independent label (distributed via Universal's Caroline). Explicitly positioned to spotlight producers Zeds Dead personally admire and promote rising talent, not just already-established names. No public demo process found — getting noticed here often happens through socials/reposts as much as cold submission.
- **Sable Valley** — RL Grime's label, launched 2019. Focuses on the "post-dubstep landscape," spans trap, future bass, DnB, dubstep, bass house. Known for promoting emerging producers. Less thematically specific to psychedelic/organic than the others here, but real and active.

## Long shot right now, not worth active pursuit yet

These surfaced through PEEKABOO's label history but are realistically out of reach for a cold submission at current audience size (~200 Spotify monthly listeners, no booking agent/manager yet — see PLATFORMS.md baseline). Worth knowing they exist, not worth spending effort on now. Revisit if the audience and buzz grow substantially.

- **OWSLA** — Skrillex's label. Technically still active, but has been quiet on new artist signings for years; historically a home for artists who were already breaking out.
- **mau5trap** — deadmau5's label. Also a genre mismatch independent of tier — house/techno/electro focused, not bass music.
- **Major Recordings / Warner Records** — a literal major label. These deals happen through management and legal representation after an artist is already breaking, not cold demo submissions.

## Not actually a label

- **Create Music Group** — a distribution, rights management, and publishing services company (like a larger-scale DistroKid/CD Baby with royalty recovery and admin services), not a curatorial label. Relevant later if distribution or publishing administration becomes a topic, not for this kind of outreach. Flagging so it doesn't get mistaken for a taste-making target.

## General submission notes

- Private SoundCloud playlist links are the standard ask — you already have this.
- Submit unreleased music only; most labels explicitly ask for this.
- A short, specific note on why the label is a fit (not a form letter) matters more than production polish at this stage. Naming the overlap with an artist already on their roster (e.g., Of The Trees at Gravitas, ATYYA at Desert Trax) is a stronger opener than a generic pitch.
- Track more candidates here as they come up rather than letting them live only in a group chat or your notes app.
